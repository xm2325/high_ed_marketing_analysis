# Informal-chat demo notes — V5.2

## Main message

This is not only a dashboard. It is a recruitment-marketing operations prototype that links:

```text
calendar deadline
→ audience segment
→ communication playbook
→ tracked campaign link
→ CRM workflow
→ KPI and guardrail reporting
→ test design
```

## Recommended five-minute demo

### 1. Recruitment Campaign Calendar

Show that student marketing activity is planned around application deadlines, Open Days, offer-holder reply points and Clearing.

### 2. Marketing Action Centre

Choose **Improve international offer-holder conversion**. Explain:

- audience: international offer-holders with no reply and confirmed consent;
- trigger: offer made + no reply + consent confirmed;
- actions: fee-value guidance, accommodation webinar, subject-level content, final reminder and adviser queue;
- primary KPI: firm-choice conversion;
- guardrails: opt-out rate and adviser workload;
- evaluation: randomised A/B test or phased rollout using approved internal records.

Then download the action-plan CSV or ICS calendar.

### 3. CRM Follow-up Queue

Show how consent checks, invalid email checks, missing course-interest fields and adviser capacity affect the operational queue.

## Three measures to discuss
